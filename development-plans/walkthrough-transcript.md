# Havenfield walkthrough - text alternative

The silent recording demonstrates the actual application in an isolated local database. Every person, property and article used in it is fictional. The publisher account shown is a disposable test identity. No password is displayed.

1. **Home:** the landing page introduces Havenfield and its original architectural concept imagery.
2. **Discovery:** the collection displays four homes. Selecting Woodland narrows the collection to Woodland Pavilion; filters are reflected in the page URL.
3. **Property and brochure:** The Courtyard House opens with its gallery. The next-photograph control changes the view. Its brochure combines the current specifications, image and original illustrative arrangement with print/PDF controls. The diagram is not a measured plan.
4. **Property editing:** the private studio opens the courtyard property's editor. Its introduction is edited and saved as published through the real versioned API.
5. **Article publication:** a new story, Light, space and everyday living, receives a title, introduction and Markdown body. Publish now saves the article through the publishing service. Search metadata, revisions and public visibility belong to the same editorial workflow.
6. **Media:** the private media library shows the image management workspace. The public tour separately explains repository assets, image descriptions, focal points, crops and reference protection.
7. **Inquiry:** the inbox filters to Alex Example's invented request. Its conversation stage becomes Viewing requested and an internal note asks for the illustrative brochure and floor plan. Saving persists the note. No message is sent and no appointment is booked.
8. **Public tour:** the read-only studio tour introduces the four workflows and displays its fixed inquiry example. The recording finishes at the public collection.
